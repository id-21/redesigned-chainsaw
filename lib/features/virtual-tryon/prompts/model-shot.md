# Model Shot Generation

Generate a professional model shot featuring the provided product.

## Scene Requirements:
- **Setting**: Clean studio environment with professional backdrop
- **Background**: Solid color or subtle gradient (white, gray, or soft pastels)
- **Lighting**: Three-point studio lighting setup for even, professional illumination
- **Model**: Age and style appropriate for product demographic
- **Composition**: Product clearly visible and featured prominently

## Model Guidelines:
- Natural, confident pose that showcases the product
- Professional appearance and styling
- Appropriate attire that complements (not competes with) the product
- Neutral or friendly expression
- Proper product interaction (wearing, holding, or using naturally)
- Model should enhance product appeal without overshadowing it

## Photography Style:
- Commercial e-commerce photography aesthetic
- Catalog/lookbook quality
- Clean, minimalist approach
- Sharp focus on product with slight background blur if needed
- Professional color grading
- High-end fashion or commercial photography standards

## Product Placement:
- Center the product in the composition
- Ensure all product details are clearly visible
- Proper fit and scale if product is wearable
- No obstruction of key features or design elements
- Product should be the primary focal point

## Lighting and Technical:
- Even, flattering lighting on both model and product
- No harsh shadows on the product
- Proper white balance and color accuracy
- Professional studio quality
- Clean, crisp image with good contrast
