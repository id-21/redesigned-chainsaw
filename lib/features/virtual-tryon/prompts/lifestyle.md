# Lifestyle Image Generation

Generate a lifestyle image showing the product in authentic, everyday use.

## Scene Requirements:
- **Setting**: Realistic environment matching product use case
- **Context**: Natural, believable scenario that demonstrates product value
- **Lighting**: Natural or ambient lighting (daylight, indoor lighting, golden hour)
- **People**: Optional, but if included, age and style appropriate for target demographic
- **Composition**: Product integrated naturally into the scene, not forced or artificial

## Environment Guidelines:

### For Apparel/Fashion:
- Urban street scenes, cafés, parks, home settings, offices
- Natural activities like walking, meeting friends, working, relaxing
- Authentic fashion styling and coordination

### For Home Goods/Decor:
- Living spaces in active use (living room, bedroom, kitchen)
- Real-life moments (reading, entertaining, cooking, relaxing)
- Lived-in, comfortable environments

### For Tech Products:
- Workspaces, commuting scenarios, leisure settings
- Active use scenarios (working, communicating, creating)
- Modern, relevant contexts

### For Accessories:
- On-the-go situations, social settings, travel contexts
- Practical use demonstrations
- Everyday moments and activities

## Authenticity Guidelines:
- Create realistic, lived-in environments (not overly staged)
- Use natural lighting and shadows
- Show genuine use case scenarios
- Include relatable, aspirational settings
- Demonstrate cultural diversity and inclusivity
- Avoid overly perfect or artificial-looking scenes

## Photography Style:
- Documentary/editorial photography aesthetic
- Warm, inviting atmosphere
- Candid, natural moments (not overly posed)
- Good depth of field to separate subject from background
- Real-world color palette with natural tones
- Lifestyle magazine or Instagram-worthy quality

## Product Presence:
- Product should be clearly visible but naturally integrated
- Show product in active use or natural display
- Context should support product value proposition
- Product fits seamlessly into the lifestyle scenario
- Demonstrates how product enhances daily life

## Emotional Tone:
- Relatable and aspirational
- Authentic and genuine
- Positive and inviting
- Demonstrates product benefits through context
- Creates emotional connection with viewer
